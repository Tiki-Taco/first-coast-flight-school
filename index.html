Who We Are

Your Local Aviation Partner

CFI & CFIICertified Flight Instructors

At First Coast Flight School, we believe aviation isn't just for those chasing a career in the cockpit. Whether you want to earn your Private Pilot License for weekend adventures, pursue an Instrument Rating, build toward a Commercial certificate, or simply experience the thrill of flight — we're here for you.

Based at Jacksonville Executive Airport (KCRG), our instructors bring professionalism, patience, and genuine passion to every lesson. We tailor our approach to your schedule, your learning style, and your goals — no pressure, no one-size-fits-all curriculum.

Training in the Cessna 172 — one of the safest and most trusted training aircraft in the world — you'll build real skills in a comfortable, capable cockpit right here on Florida's First Coast.

Transparent Rates

Simple, Honest Pricing

No surprise fees. You pay for flight time and instruction — that's it. Both billed per Hobbs hour.

$170–$210
Per Hour — Aircraft

Wet rate (fuel included), billed by Hobbs meter. Rate varies by aircraft — see our fleet for individual rates.

$60
Per Hour — Instructor

FAA Certified Flight Instructor (CFI / CFII) — expertise and patience in every lesson.

$230–$270
Per Hour — Combined

Aircraft + instructor together — your all-in rate for dual instruction. Varies by aircraft chosen.

Estimate your PPL cost: The FAA minimum is 40 flight hours, though the national average is 60–70 hours. At $230–$270/hr combined, a 40-hour minimum comes to approximately $9,200–$10,800. Contact us for a personalized estimate based on your pace and aircraft choice.
